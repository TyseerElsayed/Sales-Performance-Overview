Project Overview:
Developed an interactive Excel sales dashboard to analyze business performance across time, geography, and product categories.
The project focused on identifying revenue drivers, profitability patterns, seasonal trends, and top-performing products using Pivot Tables, KPIs, and data visualization techniques.

Key Results:
•Achieved $85.27M in total revenue and $32.22M in profit, with a strong 30% profit margin.
•Identified clear seasonality, with peak sales in December and mid-year slowdowns.
•Discovered that the United States is the leading profit contributor, indicating high geographic concentration.
•Determined that the Bikes category drives the majority of revenue, while other categories show growth potential.
•Found that a small group of products contributes disproportionately to total revenue, reflecting a Pareto-like distribution.

Challenges & Solutions:
Challenge 1: Data spread across multiple dimensions (time, geography, product). Solution: Structured the dataset using Pivot Tables and slicers to create dynamic, interactive filtering.
Challenge 2: Turning raw numbers into meaningful business insights. Solution: Designed KPI cards and insight-driven chart titles to focus on performance interpretation rather than descriptive reporting.
Challenge 3: Avoiding dashboard clutter. Solution: Applied a clean layout structure (KPIs → Performance Charts → Product Insights) with consistent formatting and limited color palette.

Lesson Learned:
• The importance of combining descriptive analysis with performance evaluation.
• How KPI selection directly impacts decision-making clarity.
• The value of storytelling in dashboards to transform data into actionable insights.
• Maintaining visual simplicity improves both usability and professional presentation.
